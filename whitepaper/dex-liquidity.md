# 6. DEX & Liquidity

## On-chain DEX
GMMT has a native DEX (**IvorySwap**). Listing and trading a token on-chain is straightforward — deploy the token and provide liquidity.

## Global liquidity
To broaden access, GMMT plans a **bridge** to major chains (e.g. Ethereum/BSC), enabling listing on **major DEXs** and improving discoverability and depth.

## Data presence
Keep information current on market-data aggregators (CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, DeFiLlama) as liquidity and products come online.
