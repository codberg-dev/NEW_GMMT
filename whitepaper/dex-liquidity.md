# 6. DEX & Liquidity

## On-chain DEX
GMMT has a native DEX (**IvorySwap**). Listing and trading a token on-chain is straightforward — deploy the token and provide liquidity.

## Global liquidity
A **bridge is currently operating**. Building on it, GMMT aims to broaden access to major chains (e.g. Ethereum/BSC), enabling listing on **major DEXs** and improving discoverability and depth. Target external DEXs and market-making strategy are being finalized.

## Data presence
Keep information current on market-data aggregators (CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, DeFiLlama) as liquidity and products come online.
