# 8. Governance & Transparency

Rebuilding trust is central to the relaunch.

- **Transparent progress:** regular, honest updates on delivery — including the relaunch itself.
- **On-chain verifiability:** asset flows and key actions verifiable via the explorer.
- **Governance:** a **staking-based voting** model is planned, giving stakers a say in key decisions, with details to be defined as it rolls out.
