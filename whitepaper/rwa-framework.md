# 3. RWA Framework

GMMT is a **multi-asset RWA chain**: the framework is asset-agnostic, so a wide range of real-world assets can be represented, verified, and transferred on-chain. Rather than committing to a single vertical, GMMT provides the common rails — issuance, verification, custody integration, and price/oracle support — that any asset type can plug into.

## What can come on-chain
The framework is designed to support many asset categories. Examples include:

| Example category | On-chain approach (concept) | Common requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commodities & minerals** | Tokens/certificates linked to underlying value | Price oracle, custody/clearing |
| **Bonds & fixed income** | On-chain representation for access & transparency | Issuer, regulatory compliance |
| **Real estate & receivables** | Fractional, transferable claims | Appraisal/audit, legal structure |
| **Other real-world assets** | Case-by-case tokenization on the same rails | Verification & custody partners |

These are illustrative — the point is flexibility. Any asset with clear ownership, valuation, and custody can be brought on-chain through the same framework.

## Principles

- **No overstatement** of returns, partners, or timelines.
- Transparency and verifiability first.
