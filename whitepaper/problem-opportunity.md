# 1. Problem & Opportunity

## Why now
GMMT's token is **fully unlocked and in circulation**. This removes future unlock overhang — upside now depends purely on demand and delivery — but it also means **constant sell pressure** without a compelling narrative and real progress. With the price at multi-year lows, the opportunity for **re-rating** is significant if the project delivers.

## Why RWA
The market is shifting from purely speculative tokens toward assets backed by **real-world value**. GMMT is well positioned to pivot from its earlier "WEB3 life" positioning to a **multi-asset Real-World Asset (RWA)** strategy — a chain that can bring any real-world asset on-chain.

## The double-edged truth
- **Strength:** full circulation, live mainnet, wallet, explorer, DEX, exchange listings.
- **Weakness:** legacy positioning, dormant dApps, deferred modernization, eroded trust.

**Thesis:** *Don't rebuild from scratch — modernize, reactivate, and redefine around real value.*
