# 9. Compliance & Risk

- **Sell pressure:** with full circulation, sustained rebound depends on real delivery and credible communication.
- **RWA regulation:** tokenizing bonds/commodities may trigger securities/financial regulation by jurisdiction — **compliance must precede launch**.
- **Core-upgrade risk:** validator coordination and hard forks carry network risk — validate thoroughly on testnet.
- **No overstatement:** unconfirmed partners, returns, or dates are never presented as fact.
